Relay K1 / K2: how to place (decision 2026-11-11: split terminals, turned 270 degrees)

Place each relay at rotation 270. The COM pin and the two coil pins then face up, NO / NC face down. Pad positions from the relay origin at rotation 270 (x, y mm):

PinNetx, y mm
5COM0.00, +7.10
1RLY_D (coil)-6.00, +5.10
4+5V (coil)+6.00, +5.10
2NO-6.00, -7.10
3NC+6.00, -7.10

COM sits 3.5 mm (pad edge to pad edge) from each coil pad, so each relay gets two slots, 8 x 1 mm with round ends, drawn on the Board Outline layer:

SlotCentre (x, y) mmAngleEnd 1End 2
A (between COM and pin 1, RLY_D)-3.00, +6.10108.4 deg(-1.74, +2.31)(-4.26, +9.89)
B (between COM and pin 4, +5V)+3.00, +6.1071.6 deg(+1.74, +2.31)(+4.26, +9.89)

Creepage COM to coil pad around a slot end: 7.4 mm (goal 6.4 mm). Keep copper (also pours) 0.5 mm from every slot.

Routing rules for the relay

  • COM leaves the pad downward, between the two slots and under the relay body, straight to the middle pin of J4 / J5.
  • NO / NC go straight down to the outer pins of J4 / J5 below the relay body.
  • Coil: pin 1 (RLY_D) and pin 4 (+5V) go up into the low-voltage strip above the relay (Q1 / D1 / R18 / R10 / LED3 / C17). Keep that strip at least 6.4 mm (7.8 mm centre to centre) above the COM pad.
  • No mains track may enter the strip, no low-voltage track may go below the relay: nothing crosses.

DECIDED Main slot and relay position

The long main slot is shortened to the HLK-5M05 area only: x = 28.1 to 29.4 mm, y = 51 to 77 mm. It separates the AC pin column of U2 from its DC column. The relays sit below it (K1 at x = 17.5, K2 at x = 39.5, y = 27 mm) and are isolated by their own slots, so no slot crosses a relay. Between the mains area (left, J2 / J3, U2 AC side, J4, J5) and the low-voltage area keep 6.4 mm by clearance where there is no slot.

Why COM in the middle: on the SRD relay the coil column lies between COM and NO / NC. With the terminal order NO / COM / NC the three mains tracks run straight down and nothing crosses.
