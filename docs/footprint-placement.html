Relay K1 / K2 isolation slots

Relay SRD-05VDC-SL-C: coil pins 1 (RLY_D) and 4 (+5V) at (-5.1, -6) and (-5.1, +6), COM pin 5 at (-7.1, 0), NO pin 2 at (+7.1, -6), NC pin 3 at (+7.1, +6) mm from the relay origin at rotation 0. COM sits 3.5 mm (pad edge to pad edge) from each coil pad, so each relay gets two slots, 8 x 1 mm with round ends:

Creepage COM to coil pad around a slot end: 7.4 mm (goal 6.4 mm). Coordinates are relative to the relay origin at rotation 0; rotate them with the relay. Layer: Board Outline, like the main isolation slot. Keep copper (and pours) 0.5 mm from every slot. COM leaves to the left between the slots, coil nets leave up (pin 4) and down (pin 1).

DECISION Relay position vs the main isolation slot

The planned floor plan puts each relay across the long main slot (x 28.1 to 29.4 mm, y 7.0 to 77.0 mm), contacts on the mains side and coil on the low-voltage side. The real pin layout does not allow that: COM is on the coil side of the relay, and the coil column lies between COM and NO / NC. With the relay at 180 degrees COM and the coil are both on the low-voltage side; at 0 degrees the coil is on the mains side. Either way a mains or coil net would have to cross the slot, which a continuous slot forbids.

Options

  1. Shorten the main slot to the HLK-5M05 area only (y about 50 to 78 mm, so it still separates the AC and DC pin columns of U2), and give each relay its own two slots. The relays then sit left of x about 40 mm with J4 / J5 on the left edge; the coil nets (RLY_D, +5V) reach them from the right and the NO / NC nets must pass round the coil end with 6.4 mm clearance.
  2. Move J4 / J5 so COM and NO / NC come out on different sides (two terminals instead of one 3-way), which avoids routing round the coil. Needs a BOM change.
  3. Change the relay to one with COM and NO / NC on the same side and the coil on the other, then keep the long slot as planned. Needs a new footprint and a BOM change.

Option 1 needs no part change and is what I would try first. Tell me which one you choose and I update the floor plan and the table below.
