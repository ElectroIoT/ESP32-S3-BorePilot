Circuit view

How 5 V and 3.3 V are made. Colours: orange = 5 V, yellow = 3.3 V, grey = GND, green = GPIO, pink = display / I2C, purple = USB, cyan = UART, red / blue = mains.

  1. The HLK output and USB VBUS each go through a Schottky diode into the common +5V rail.
  2. C1, C5, C6 smooth the rail; C2 sits on the LDO input.
  3. U3 makes +3V3, with C3 at its output.
