Circuit view

How the mains reaches the HLK module. The red dashed area is the 230 V side. Colours: orange = 5 V, yellow = 3.3 V, grey = GND, green = GPIO, pink = display / I2C, purple = USB, cyan = UART, red / blue = mains.

  1. J2 pin 1 is L, pin 2 is N. J3 repeats L and N for the PZEM voltage input.
  2. U2 turns it into isolated 5 V (+5V_HLK) on the low-voltage side.
  3. There is no fuse or varistor on the board: your panel breaker must protect it.
