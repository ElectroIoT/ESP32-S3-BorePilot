Relay K1 / K2: slots around the coil pins

Decision 2026-11-11: cut two slots in each relay footprint, one between COM (pin 5) and each coil pin (pins 1 and 4), so the mains pin and the 5 V coil are separated by a longer path through the board.

  • Coordinates are relative to the relay origin with the relay at rotation 0 (y up, as in the EasyEDA PCB). Rotate the slots with the relay.
  • Creepage is measured pad edge to pad edge along the shortest path round the slot end: 7.4 mm against the 6.4 mm goal in PLAN.md (a 7 mm slot would give 6.6 mm, 6 mm only 5.9 mm).
  • Slot edge to the nearest pad: 1.27 mm. The slots lie under the relay body, so the relay does not need extra board area.
  • Routing rules: COM leaves to the left through the gap between the slots; coil pins leave up (pin 4) and down (pin 1), never across a slot. NO / NC are 12.2 mm from the coil, no slot needed.
  • Make the slots on the Board Outline layer like the existing isolation slot, and no copper (also no pour) within 0.5 mm of them.
  • This is a board-level measure. The relay itself still needs its own coil-to-contact rating checked, and the whole mains section needs the engineer review in PLAN.md section 14.
